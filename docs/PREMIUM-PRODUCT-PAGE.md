# Premium product page

Replaces the old cream/yellow utility modal with a dark product page: large fixed front-view portrait, compact colour swatches, price summary, lifetime ownership details and one Apple purchase action. The selected vehicle and colour are announced accessibly. Phone landscape keeps the preview, finishes, price, purchase action and Back visible together. The backdrop is blurred and dimmed to keep unrelated game messages from competing with checkout.

In the browser the proposed AUD price is identified as a proposal and the purchase button is disabled. In the native app only a returned StoreKit price enables buying. Apple approval, cancellation, restore and optional owned-car upgrades continue through the verified transaction handlers. The product-page redesign does not activate live products or replace the release requirements in APPLE-CASH-PACKS.md.

## Rendered colour variants

Built-in image generation tool; final asset `assets/premium-colours-v3.webp`. A transparent 4×4 atlas with fourteen rendered portraits, rather than tint overlays. Red/yellow pairs: Volt at 0/1, Apex 2/3, Phantom 4/5, Nova 6/7, Circuit 8/9, Rocket X 10/11, Ferrari 458 12/13. Final two cells intentionally empty. Circuit's race-prototype portrait cannot resolve to Rocket X's hypercar frame.

Prompt: Match the reference front three-quarter showroom camera and polished realistic graphic-novel style. Four equal columns and rows, fourteen complete isolated cars, transparent margins, no labels or environment. Each model has identical red and bright factory-yellow versions side by side, metallic wheels, dark glass, black tyres; clean uniform body paint with no leftover red in yellow versions. Row one: smooth electric supercar (Volt), angular wedge supercar (Apex). Row two: long-nose aero hypercar (Phantom), rounded winged hypercar (Nova). Row three: Le Mans prototype racer (Circuit), futuristic hypercar (Rocket X). Row four: Ferrari 458, then two transparent cells. No overhead views, muddy gold finishes or clipped vehicles.
