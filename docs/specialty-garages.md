# Specialty garages update

Implemented locally on 7 November 2026.

- Walk-in performance, chassis and security garages replace the remote warehouse workbench.
- Walk to an engine/turbo, tyres/armour or security bench to book the relevant work. The service lift handles repairs and collection; the office handles workshop progression.
- Each garage saves its own equipment level. Level 1 fits basic upgrades; level 2 unlocks intermediate upgrades and mechanic hiring; level 3 unlocks the full upgrade range.
- Current balancing uses in-game money: equipment upgrades cost $1,000 then $4,000; hiring Maya costs $1,500 once per garage. Labour is $60 without Maya or $20 with her, in addition to parts. Fitting takes nine seconds, or four with Maya.
- Jobs remain saved when leaving or reloading, and must be collected at the lift. Vehicles under service cannot be driven away before collection.
- Premium vehicles and their paid upgrades retain the Apple App Store purchase route in AUD.
- A new four-panel workshop comic introduces the garages, with follow-up chapters for equipment upgrades and hiring Maya.

Validation: 16 automated tests; 35 existing gameplay checks; seven garage browser checks; 340 comic panel/viewport combinations across iPhone Max and iPad. All passed. Screen fitting was tested in simulated browser sizes, not on physical devices.
