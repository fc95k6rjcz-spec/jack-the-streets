# Playable 200-vehicle fleet

The catalogue contains 200 selectable, purchasable vehicles: 28 retained models, four commercial models now available to buy, and 168 additions. Existing IDs, ownership and upgrade saves stay stable. Each model has a deterministic procedural engine voice; these are synthesized sounds, not manufacturer recordings.

All new overhead artwork is generated using the built-in image generation tool. Nine category atlases plus the specialist sheet, six premium designs and four commercial vehicles are in assets. Cell coordinates are in fleet-detail-manifest.inc.js. The production renderer rotates noses from up to right and isolates each vehicle's connected body to avoid neighbouring frame fragments. Existing front-view portraits remain available; additions use their overhead artwork in the showroom.

## Controls and behavior

- Dealership: search by name/type, filter by vehicle category, and use Hear Engine for a four-second preview.
- Workshop: Get In places the selected saved car at its physical position in the rendered workshop; steering and throttle drive through the roller door and forecourt. The workshop remains visible in the world.
- Weapon: H or Draw/Holster button. New weapons start holstered. Drawing in a shop raises an alarm and robbery intent once per visit. Holstering preserves ammunition and does not undo an alarm.
- Damage: directional cosmetic damage, frontal engine damage and side-impact alignment damage persist with owned cars. Serious side impacts can puncture tyres. Power/grip deteriorate with mechanical damage; repairs clear the systems alongside body damage.
