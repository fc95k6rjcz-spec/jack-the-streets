# Signature car designs

Three original wrap designs follow the supplied high-detail racing-car references:

- **Gold Fang** — Subaru STI: yellow leopard-pattern bonnet, blue/white body, gold wheels and original big-cat mask graphics.
- **Neon Ronin** — Ferrari 458: pearl white, black aero and magenta inkwork with an original mask design.
- **Crimson Drift** — Rocket X: white and black, crimson brushwork, red-rim wheels and dramatic aero.

The showroom has a Signature Designs filter. Owning the relevant car unlocks its included wrap; fitting and restoring factory paint are reversible and do not charge additional currency. Unowned cars can preview the designs. The premium product page has Factory/Signature choices and preserves the selected design after verified Apple ownership delivery. The free starter remains the Rustbucket.

Each design has a paired front portrait and overhead driving frame. The renderer checks the saved `look.signature` value, and the sprite cache includes it, so a fitted design appears on the actual player car and its parked version rather than just in the purchase menu. The look is stored with the existing saved profile. This change does not add new Apple products or activate live payments.
