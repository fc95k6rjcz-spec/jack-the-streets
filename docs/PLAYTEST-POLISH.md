# Playtest changes

- Character selection uses cinematic front portraits and accepts a saved street name. Alternate characters have dedicated opening, mission and workshop comic scenes; captions and protagonist dialogue use the selected name.
- Coastal Car Yard and Harbour Performance are physical destinations with signs, GPS entries and a Browse Cars action that opens the existing showroom.
- BuildMate (formerly Bunnings) has a direct free garage-pack collection button and return-home guidance.
- Minor vehicle bumps do not trigger reports. A closing impact of 240 game units or more qualifies, with an eight-second report cooldown. Pedestrian injuries remain reportable.
- Arrests confiscate unspent bank loot, then charge up to $1,000 from earned money. Purchased cash is tracked and protected. No negative balance. Old purchase-ledger saves conservatively protect their existing clean balance. Campaign collision/coin payouts are removed and stunt payouts reduced to one-fifth; mission and race payouts remain.
- Engine voices are procedural synthesis, not recordings: each model gets its own harmonic profile. Rustbucket adds idle instability and mechanical rattle; the Rosso V8 has a much higher rev range. Mute/pause/interior conditions mute texture layers too.
- The Rosso V8 (previous Ferrari-labelled car) now uses stock speed/acceleration/grip multipliers 1.65/1.85/1.42. Damage, fuel and tyres still affect performance.
- New overhead police sprite, rendered boutique interiors, thinner muted GPS, textured motorway decks with matching road width, smaller pursuit cards and a dedicated custody view.
- Businesses and vehicle display names are fictionalised. This is not an intellectual-property clearance: existing artwork, badges, sound licensing and store metadata still require review before release.
