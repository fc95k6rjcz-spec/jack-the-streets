# Career and garage quality pass

This is a local quality pass, not a claim of a five-star finished game. No deployment was made.

- Garage cars now render beneath the roller-door beam and jambs. The foreground uses the existing artwork, with no new collision wall in the opening.
- Dispatch recommends nearby jobs across different activity types. Legal and high-risk filters, pickup distances, earnings labels and time estimates make the choices easier to understand. Locked jobs no longer fill the available-work list.
- Cancellation takes a second confirmation click. Current story objectives stay protected.
- Context-sensitive objectives explain stopping, boarding the cargo truck, entering the shop, losing police and race checkpoint progress. Police speech takes priority over the contract strip.
- World destination rings match the existing pickup/drop-off and race acceptance distances.
- Completed and failed jobs produce saved receipts with actual money paid, damage, time remaining and a contact response. A result button opens the receipt without interrupting driving; it links to the next job. Payments and prices are unchanged.
- District text reads “Cruising Newport”, without the extra “the”.

Verification: 101 Node checks passed; focused Chromium checks exercised legal filtering, completion, single payment, receipt totals, phone menu overflow and garage layering, with no page errors. Nine existing gameplay browser suites were rerun. Browser contexts used isolated saves.

Still outside this pass: replacement of all art, full directional indoor sprites, individually authored stories for all 300 routes, physical iPad testing and a full real-time playthrough of every route. See the 30-point review for the earlier fixes and remaining art limitations.
